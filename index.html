Start with the included examples

  1. Open a sample project on the home screen.
  2. Choose Practice for feedback after submitting, or Mock exam for a timed session.
  3. Review the results, then export your responses if you want to discuss them with an external tool.

How do I import my own questions?

Create a custom exam project and choose Import question set. Rehearse accepts rehearse.quiz version 1 JSON, pasted or selected from Files. The Tools screen has a reusable prompt for an external AI tool to help format source questions. Check the question count, answer key and material before confirming; add missing question images if needed.

PDFs, handwritten notes and photos are not automatically converted into questions inside Rehearse. External AI services are separate from the app and may have their own accounts, fees and privacy policies.

What is included for free?

One custom project and two custom question banks in total across the entire app. The allowance is not two banks per project. Unmodified built-in examples do not count; editing an example makes it custom content. Practice, mock exams, written responses, history, export and backups are available within the free allowance.

What does Full Unlock do?

A one-time non-consumable in-app purchase removes the project and question-bank count limits. There is no subscription. The price appears in your App Store currency. This purchase does not include an external AI service or guarantee exam results.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use Settings → Full Unlock → Restore purchases, using the Apple Account that made the purchase. An App Store connection is required. If approval is pending, the free limits remain until Apple confirms. Cancellation does not change your saved content. If a purchase is revoked, existing saved work stays available; further additions beyond the allowance need an active unlock.

If the App Store product is temporarily unavailable, continue using the free features and try again later. Refund requests are handled by Apple.

Where are my data and backups?

Study content is saved locally in the app on your iPhone. Export a complete backup in Settings before changing devices or deleting the app. iOS device backups may include app data according to your device settings; Rehearse does not provide its own automatic cloud sync.

Restoring a compatible rehearse.backup version 1 file validates it first and keeps recovery copies before replacing your current data. Cancelled or invalid restores do not overwrite your content. A backup that exceeds the free allowance requires unlocking unless it fits the quantities of content already saved on this installation. Recovery copies can be exported from Settings.

If saving fails, keep the app open and export a backup immediately. The app confirms a save only after it succeeds.

Are written answers graded?

No. Written responses are preserved verbatim and can be exported for your own review or an external AI tool. Objective results use the answer key you imported; questions without a key are not automatically marked correct.

Can I use it offline?

Stored question sets, practice, mock exams, history and exports work offline. Buying or restoring purchases, visiting this support site and using an external AI tool require a connection.

Which interface languages are available?

English (default), Simplified Chinese, Spanish, Japanese, German, French, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese and Traditional Chinese. Changing the interface language does not translate or rewrite your imported content or written responses. This support site is provided in English.
