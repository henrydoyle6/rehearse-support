Privacy policy · Effective 8 October 2026

Your study content
stays under your control.

This policy covers Rehearse-mock test for iPhone, provided by HANWEI LI, and this support website.

Content stored on your device

Projects, question banks, question images, session snapshots, written responses, history and preferences are stored in the app locally. Rehearse does not send this study content to a developer server. It has no Rehearse account, advertising SDK, tracking SDK or integrated AI API.

iOS system backups may include app data according to your device and iCloud settings. Those backups are managed by Apple. Deleting the app may remove its local data; keep an exported backup if you need to retain it.

Files, images, copying and sharing

File and image access occurs when you choose a file or image through the system picker. The app does not scan your photo library or other files. Selected question images become part of the local question bank and may appear in a backup or an export you choose.

Rehearse copies text to the clipboard only when you choose a copy action. It does not continuously monitor the clipboard. Files and text leave the app when you select a system sharing destination or manually paste them into another service. Review exported data before sharing. An export or full backup may contain question content, answers and written responses.

External AI tools

The app supplies reusable prompts and exports for use with external AI tools. It does not make AI requests itself. If you send content to an AI provider, that provider handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. Avoid sharing personal or confidential material unnecessarily.

Purchases

Full Unlock uses Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple manages payment information and your Apple Account. Rehearse checks verified purchase entitlements on the device to enable the unlock, restore it and respond to revocation. The developer does not receive or store payment-card details and does not operate a separate purchase-account backend.

Support and this website

If you contact support by email, the developer receives your email address and the contents and attachments you choose to send, and uses them to respond and investigate the issue. Do not send passwords or verification codes. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable record-retention obligation.

This static support site uses no analytics scripts, advertising trackers or tracking cookies added by the developer. It is intended to be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses for hosting and security under GitHub’s privacy statement. Email delivery is handled by the relevant email providers.

Your choices

You can export your study data and delete individual projects, question banks or sessions in the app. Historic session snapshots are separate: deleting a bank does not delete its session history. Delete sessions separately if desired. Restoring a backup preserves pre-restore recovery copies on the device. Removing the app removes its app storage subject to iOS backup behavior. Copies exported to Files, email or another service are controlled by you and that service.

Children and updates

Rehearse has no child-profile registration or age-based advertising. The developer does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. A parent or guardian can contact support about information voluntarily sent by email. Changes to data handling will be reflected in this policy with an updated effective date.
